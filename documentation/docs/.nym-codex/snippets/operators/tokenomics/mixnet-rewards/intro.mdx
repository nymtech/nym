* Nym tokenomics are based on the research paper [*Reward Sharing for Mixnets*](https://nym.com/nym-cryptoecon-paper.pdf)
* For a more comprehensive overview, live data and supply graphs, visit [*explorer.nym.spectredao.net/token*](https://explorer.nym.spectredao.net/token)

We are working on the final architecture of [*Fair Mixnet*](snippet:nym/operators/tokenomics/mixnet-rewards/fair-mixnet) tokenomics implementation and its detailed documentation. **The current design is called [*Naive rewarding*](snippet:nym/operators/tokenomics/mixnet-rewards/naive-rewarding).** It is an intermediate step, allowing operators to migrate to `nym-node` in Mixnet smart contract and for the first time receive delegations and earn rewards for any `nym-node` [functionality](../nodes/nym-node/setup#functionality-mode), in opposite to the past system, where only Mixnodes were able to receive delegations and rewards.

**Please read the [roadmap section below](snippet:nym/operators/tokenomics/mixnet-rewards/roadmap) to see the planned development.**
