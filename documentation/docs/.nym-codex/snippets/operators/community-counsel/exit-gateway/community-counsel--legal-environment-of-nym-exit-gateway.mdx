## Community Counsel & Legal environment of Nym Exit Gateway [#community-counsel--legal-environment-of-nym-exit-gateway]

The Node Operators Community Counsel pages are divided according [jurisdictions](snippet:nym/operators/community-counsel/jurisdictions/index/intro). Nym Node operators are invited to add their legal findings or helpful suggestions directly through [pull requests](snippet:nym/operators/community-counsel/add-content/intro). This can be done as a new legal information (or entire new country) to the list of [jurisdictions](snippet:nym/operators/community-counsel/jurisdictions/index/intro) or in a form of an advice to [Community counsel pages](snippet:nym/operators/community-counsel/index/intro), like sharing examples of Exit Gateway [landing pages](snippet:nym/operators/community-counsel/landing-pages/intro), templates etcetra.
