# Nym Node Families

<VarInfo />

**This page is a how to guide showing steps to [create a node family](#create-node-family), [invite nodes to a family](#invite-new-node-to-a-family), [accept invitation](#accept-invitation-to-a-family), [leave a family](#leave-a-family) and to [edit a family](#edit-or-dissolve-a-family), where operators can change name, description or completely dissolve a family.**


NymVPN protects user privacy by routing traffic through decentralised Nym nodes. This only works securely if those nodes are genuinely independent: if the Entry and Exit gateways in one route are run by the different operator and located in different network subnets. Otherwise an adversary could observe connection metadata end-to-end, or to trace users through co-located infrastructure.

To address this, Nym is adopting the concept of node families — a mechanism already used by the [Tor network](https://community.torproject.org/relay/setup/post-install/family-ids/) — whereby operators who run multiple nodes cryptographically declare them as belonging to the same family. 

The routing policy then disallows connections where Entry and Exit gateways share a family, ensuring meaningful separation between them. This significantly raises the bar for a traffic correlation attack, whether from a malicious operator silently running multiple nodes or from an adversary performing infrastructure seizure within a single jurisdiction.

## Family Management

Nym node families are based on Nyx accounts, so a family can be [created](#create-node-family) by an account without any nodes, [inviting](#invite-new-node-to-a-family) them afterwards.

Therefore, the management of Nym node families is done via [Nym Wallet](/operators/nodes/preliminary-steps/wallet-preparation), alternatively [`nym-cli`](/developers/tools/nym-cli). In this how to guide we use Nym Wallet UI, in case you prefer the CLI, build it following [these steps](/developers/tools/nym-cli/usage) and then see the commands in [this section](/developers/tools/nym-cli/commands) or follow `<COMMAND> --help` convention.

Node families can be well previewed on [SpectreDAO Explorer](https://explorer.nym.spectredao.net/families).

For any operation regarding node family, open your wallet and navigate to the section *Family - Manage node families*

![](/images/operators/wallet-screenshots/fam-tab.png)

### Create Node Family

To create node family, open your wallet and follow the steps below.

<Steps>
###### 1. Ensure balance bigger than 101 NYM

- Make sure you have at least 101 NYM to use 100 NYM as refundable deposit and have a bit left for the network fees

- This refundable fee follows the same logic like [bonding](/operators/nodes/nym-node/bonding), using a little cost as spam prevention

###### 2. Navigate to the section *Family - Manage node families*

###### 3. Describe your family

- In the default tab *My Family* enter your *Family Name* and *Description*

![](/images/operators/wallet-screenshots/fam-create.png)

<Callout type="info" emoji="ℹ️">
Make sure to describe your in a concise but clear way, refraning from offensive or inside joke tyoe of descriptions. 
</Callout>

###### 4. Confirm

- Click on *Create family - 100 NYM* and then confirm

</Steps>

Your family is now created, congratulation. You can preview it from your wallet or visit [SpectreDAO Explorer](https://explorer.nym.spectredao.net/families).

![](/images/operators/wallet-screenshots/fam-overview.png)


### Invite New Node to a Family

To invite other nodes, open your wallet and follow the steps below.

<Steps>
###### 1. Navigate to the section *Family - Manage node families* 

###### 2. Fill *Invite a Node* field

- Add a `NODE-ID` of the node you want to add to the family (the digit, not node identity key)

- Make sure that you only invite nodes you control and that are already bonded and operational

![](/images/operators/wallet-screenshots/fam-create-invite.png)

###### 3. Send and confirm

- Click on *Send invite* and confirm

</Steps>

Your invite was sent, you can preview it in your wallet.

![](/images/operators/wallet-screenshots/fam-invite-preview.png)

### Accept invitation to a Family

To accept an invitation, open your wallet and follow the steps below.

<Steps>
###### 1. Navigate to the section *Family - Manage node families* 

- You will see a notification, click on the button

![](/images/operators/wallet-screenshots/fam-notifycation.png)

###### 2. Navigate to the tab *Invites*

###### 3. Review the family

<Callout type="warning" emoji="⚠️">
Remember that anyone could have invited your node to their family, review  carefully the invitation account authenticity. 
</Callout>

###### 4. Accept & confirm

- Click on *Accept* and on *Accept invite* to confirm

![](/images/operators/wallet-screenshots/fam-invite-accept.png)

</Steps>

Congratulation, your node had join a family. You can preview the node status in the wallet

![](/images/operators/wallet-screenshots/fam-node-overview.png)


### Leave a Family

In case you want to leave a family with your node, open your wallet and follow the steps below.

<Steps>
###### 1. Navigate to the section *Family - Manage node families* 

###### 2. Navigate to *My Family* tab

###### 3. Click on *Leave `<FAMILY_NAME>`* & confirm

![](/images/operators/wallet-screenshots/fam-node-overview.png)

</Steps>


### Edit or Dissolve a Family

You can edit your family name and description as well as dissolving it entirely. Open your wallet and follow the steps below.
