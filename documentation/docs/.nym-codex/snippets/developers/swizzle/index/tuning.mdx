## Tuning [#tuning]

<Callout type="warning">
**There are no settled numbers.** Wider overlaps and wider checkpoint spacing
buy a larger anonymity set, and cost re-downloaded data. The defaults are
conservative percentage-of-range derivations, exposed as knobs. Treat them as
starting points; nobody has validated them as anonymity parameters.
</Callout>

Defaults, for reference:

| Knob | Default |
|---|---|
| `chunk_size` | `[total/50, total/10]`, at least 1 |
| `overlap` | `[1, clamp(total/20, 1, max_chunk/2)]` |
| `start_jitter_default` | 10% of the total range, at least 1 |
| `floor` | 0 |
