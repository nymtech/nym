- [x] 3.2 Rolling single-claim guard for FRESH grants at the finalisation seam (`handle_final_credential_claim` -> `grant_or_resume_free_tier`), a pure `classify_free_tier_claim(record, now, time_cap, claim_window)` over the single `granted_at`.
