## Your destination port may not be permitted [#your-destination-port-may-not-be-permitted]

Every exit gateway applies the same
[Nym exit policy](https://nymtech.net/.wellknown/network-requester/exit-policy.txt),
a deny list agreed by operator governance. It decides which destination ports may
leave the mixnet, it is identical across operators, and an individual operator
cannot vary it.

Check it against the port your service listens on before anything else. It is
the one constraint that no code on your side can work around. See
[exit security](snippet:nym/developers/exit-security/intro) for which exit your package
uses.

A refused port is not final: the policy can be changed through a governance
proposal and an operator vote, described in the
[exit gateway community counsel](/operators/community-counsel/exit-gateway).
