## Why

`nym-validator-rewarder` is the off-chain process that decides who gets paid, and how much, for two distinct pieces of work securing the Nyx chain and the ecash system: signing blocks as a Nyx validator, and issuing partial ticketbooks as a DKG signer. It holds a funded mnemonic, measures both activities itself, and settles every epoch and every day with a `send_multiple` bank transaction. It is money-moving, unattended, and specified nowhere outside 4.5k lines of Rust.

The measurement rules are where the value is, and almost none of them are inferable from a single file:

- **Block signing is scored from a private chain scrape, not from the chain's own slashing view.** The binary embeds `nyxd-scraper-sqlite`, follows the chain over a websocket, and stores every pre-commit. A validator's score is `signed_blocks / blocks_in_epoch` multiplied by its share of *whitelisted* voting power, so the whitelist is simultaneously the payout allow-list and the denominator of everyone's stake share.
- **Voting power is sampled from a 20-block window at the start of the epoch, and only from there.** A validator with no stored pre-commit in `first_block..min(first_block+20, last_block)` is dropped from the epoch entirely - it earns nothing even if it signed the remaining 99% of the hour. This single window is also why the deployment cannot prune aggressively, and why the recovery runbook printed on startup failure involves manually disabling pruning.
- **Ticketbook issuance is scored by an interactive, signed challenge-response audit, with bans for wrong answers.** The rewarder discovers issuers from the DKG contract (current dealers with verified VK shares), asks each for a signed commitment (deposit-id list plus merkle root) over one expiration-date cohort, tosses a weighted coin to decide whether to audit at all, samples deposits, demands a merkle proof plus the raw blinded signatures, and verifies each partial credential against that issuer's partial key. From the challenge onward, *any* answer that is not a correct one - no response, bad signature, a tampered echo of the rewarder's own signed request, a short batch, a failing proof, a self-declared batch size below the protocol minimum - is treated as cheating and produces a ban plus a serialised, independently checkable evidence blob. That the rewarder signs its own requests is what makes the evidence self-contained, and is easy to mistake for ceremony.
- **The audited cohort is keyed by expiration date, not issuance date, and is audited one day after it expires.** With 7-day ticketbook validity and a 2-day retention window in nym-api, the day's run audits work performed roughly a week earlier, in the last day before the signers are entitled to delete the data. The timing is a three-way constraint between validity, retention and the rewarding cadence, and looks arbitrary without it.
- **The two modules share only a budget line.** Separate tickers (hourly epoch vs. daily at midnight + 2h), separate whitelists in different address formats (`nvalcons1…` consensus addresses vs. `n1…` operator accounts), separate enable and `monitor_only` switches, separate storage tables, separate resume state. Block signing under-spends its budget on downtime; issuance divides its budget into equal per-operator slices and leaves unclaimed slices unspent.
- **Several load-bearing behaviours were current-state slips that a reader would otherwise assume work; documenting them is what surfaced them, and they have since been fixed in the crate.** The audit's merkle leaf-count check compared the issuer's whole-tree leaf count against the *sample* size, banning every audited issuer holding more than the sample size and withholding the day's settlement (now compared against the committed count, alongside a related merkle-proof ordering fix). Two other audit paths paid an unverified claim outright - a commitment for the wrong expiration date (now dropped) and a commitment with no merkle root (now a ban). The persistent issuer ban could never match on a later day, because the insert's two arguments were swapped (now matched by operator account). `issued_ratio` was computed against a deposit union that grew as issuers were checked in sequence and was not capped at 1 (now order-independent and capped at 1.0). The epoch block count was `last - first` while pre-commits are counted inclusively (now inclusive). A failed rewarding transaction wrote its error text into the `rewarding_tx` column (now `rewarding_error`), and both settlement memos named the wrong period (now each names the period being paid). Each was a real property of the deployed binary; the specs above describe the fixed behaviour.

Writing this down now, while the behaviour and its rationale are recoverable, is far cheaper than reconstructing it from `git blame` the next time a validator disputes a payout - and it gives any future change to the reward formulas, the whitelists, or the audit protocol a normative baseline to diff against.

## What Changes

- Introduce three new capability specs covering the binary end to end, split along the seams the implementation already has:
  - `validator-rewarder` - the shared core: configuration and budget derivation, the module enable / `monitor-only` matrix, the pre-flight balance gate, key material, the two-ticker main loop, epoch bookkeeping and startup resync, reward transaction construction and the zero-amount guard, the sqlite audit log, and the operator CLI including the documented recovery runbook.
  - `validator-rewarder-block-signing` - the chain-scrape measurement: epoch windowing, the 20-block voting-power sampling window, whitelist gating of both eligibility and the voting-power denominator, signed-block counting, the reward formula, validator-detail resolution from the union of the historical and live validator sets, operator-account derivation by bech32 prefix swap, and the per-validator and per-epoch failure modes.
  - `validator-rewarder-ticketbook-issuance` - the audit protocol: issuer discovery from the DKG contract, cohort selection by expiration date, the pre-ban check, the issuance-count gate, the signed commitment, the full-verification coin toss, deposit sampling, the challenge and data-retrieval exchanges, the four per-ticketbook verification checks, the complete ban taxonomy and evidence format, the reward formula, and the daily minimum-deposit settlement gate.
- Document the **normative reward formulas** in both modules, including what happens to the unspent remainder in each: `budget x ratio_signed x voting_power_ratio` per validator for block signing, and `per_operator_budget x issued_ratio` for issuance, with `per_operator_budget = daily_budget x issuance_ratio / whitelist_size`.
- Document the **configuration surface and its defaults** (`daily_budget`, the three `ratios` that must sum to 1.0, `epoch_duration`, both whitelists, `min_validate_per_issuer`, `sampling_rate`, `full_verification_ratio`, `minimum_daily_ticketbooks`, scraper pruning and `store_precommits`), since every one of these knobs changes what a measurement means, and note which fields the emitted `config.toml` template omits.
- Document the **dependency contracts** the rewarder relies on but does not own: the `nyxd-scraper-sqlite` queries that define "signed a block" and "blocks in an epoch", and the four nym-api ecash endpoints (`issued-ticketbooks-for/count`, `issued-ticketbooks-for`, `issued-ticketbooks-challenge-commitment`, `issued-ticketbooks-data`) plus the merkle-leaf hashing in `nym-ticketbooks-merkle` that the audit is defined against.
- Document the **remaining known gaps** (the money-moving defects these specs originally ratified have since been fixed in the crate, and the requirements above describe the fixed behaviour): the inert `ticketbook_verification` ratio with no rewarding module, the `--epoch-budget` flag that sets the daily budget despite its name, the swapped whitelist log messages, the trivially-true midnight assertion, the `_v1` legacy tables left unread, and the `monitor_only` flag that also marks a settlement withheld by the minimum-deposit gate.
- No code changes, no migrations, no new dependencies. This is a documentation-only deliverable that ratifies the current implementation as the baseline. Behaviour a reviewer judges to be an actual bug becomes a follow-on change, not an edit to these specs.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `validator-rewarder`: Shared core of the `nym-validator-rewarder` binary - configuration and budget derivation, module enablement and monitor-only mode, balance and key pre-flight, the two-ticker main loop, epoch bookkeeping and startup resync, reward transaction construction, the sqlite audit log, and the operator CLI and recovery runbook.
- `validator-rewarder-block-signing`: Measurement and rewarding of Nyx validator block signing from the embedded nyxd scraper - epoch windowing, voting-power sampling, whitelist gating, signed-block counting, the reward formula, and epoch failure modes.
- `validator-rewarder-ticketbook-issuance`: Measurement and rewarding of ecash ticketbook issuance - issuer discovery from the DKG contract, the signed challenge-response audit over one expiration-date cohort, the ban and evidence taxonomy, the reward formula, and the daily settlement gate.

## Impact

- **Documentation only.** No runtime behaviour changes.
- **Code described** (treated as the normative baseline): `nym-validator-rewarder/src/**` in full - `rewarder/mod.rs`, `rewarder/epoch.rs`, `rewarder/nyxd_client.rs`, `rewarder/helpers.rs`, `rewarder/block_signing/{mod,types}.rs`, `rewarder/ticketbook_issuance/{mod,verifier,types,helpers}.rs`, `rewarder/storage/{mod,manager}.rs`, `config/**`, `cli/**`, `error.rs`, and the four files in `migrations/`.
- **Dependency contracts catalogued** (behaviour owned elsewhere, referenced here because the measurements are defined in terms of it): `common/nyxd-scraper-sqlite/src/storage/**` (pre-commit and block queries, pruning), `nym-api/src/ecash/api_routes/issued.rs` and `nym-api/src/ecash/state/mod.rs` (the audited endpoints, retention cutoff, maximum data response size), `common/ticketbooks-merkle/src/lib.rs` (leaf hashing and proofs), `common/ecash-time/src/lib.rs` (day and expiration-date arithmetic), `common/network-defaults/src/ecash.rs` (`TICKETBOOK_VALIDITY_DAYS`, `MINIMUM_TICKETBOOK_DATA_REQUEST_SIZE`), and the `nym-coconut-dkg` contract queries used for issuer discovery.
- **No migrations. No new dependencies.** The sqlite schema is described as-is, including the `_v1`-suffixed tables left behind by the pre-revamp combined-rewarding era.
