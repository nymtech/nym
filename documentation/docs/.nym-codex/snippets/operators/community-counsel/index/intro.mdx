Running an Exit Gateway is a commitment and as such is exposed to various challenges. Besides different legal regulations typical difficulties may be dealing with VPS or ISP providers. Our strength lies in decentralised community of squads and individuals supporting each other. Sharing examples of [landing pages](snippet:nym/operators/community-counsel/landing-pages/intro), templates for communication and FAQs is a great way to empower other operators sharing the mission of liberating internet. [How to add content](snippet:nym/operators/community-counsel/index/how-to-add-content) shows a simple way to create a pull request directly to the Nym Operator Guide docs.
