## There is no address discovery [#there-is-no-address-discovery]

This is deliberate: clients should not be enumerable, so there is no directory,
no DHT and no lookup service for Nym addresses. Addresses are exchanged out of
band. An application ships its service provider's address in its configuration
instead of resolving it at runtime.

If your design assumes clients can find each other by name, that mechanism is
yours to build.
