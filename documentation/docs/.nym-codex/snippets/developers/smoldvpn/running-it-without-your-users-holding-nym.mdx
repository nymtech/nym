### Running it without your users holding NYM [#running-it-without-your-users-holding-nym]

Your end-users do not have to acquire or hold NYM. Run the [`nym-credential-proxy`](https://github.com/nymtech/nym/tree/develop/nym-credential-proxy/nym-credential-proxy), an authenticated service you operate that issues zk-nyms on their behalf: your app authenticates to the proxy, the proxy issues the unlinkable credentials, and your users get Nym access without handling any tokens.
